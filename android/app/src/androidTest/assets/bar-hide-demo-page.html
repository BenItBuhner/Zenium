The bar that hides on scroll

A demo page served from inside the browser's own process. Scroll down and the address bar slides off its edge one to one with the page; scroll up a little and it comes back; let go part way and it snaps whichever way is nearer.

The box below scrolls on its own. A finger inside it scrolls the box and moves no bar, as in Chrome; the bar follows the page's own scroll only.

The blue band at the bottom is fixed to the viewport's bottom edge and reads the viewport's height. When the bar has gone the page has its band, so the number grows by the band's height and the blue edge sits where the bar was; when the bar is back the number is what it was.
